*In brief: A goblin-bugbear-ogre raid struck Golden Fields in the night mist, and the crew fought through two waves — inn-yard, then burning grove — before the abbey bell tolled to warn that giants were coming.*

## Waking to a Raid

The crew had bedded down at Miros' inn after a long day in Golden Fields — the yeti innkeeper Miros, the noble wizard Naxene, the drunken halfling bard Orin, and a quiet stranger named Shalvis all under the same roof, Shalvis sharing a room with Kella, the estranged Zhentarim bounty the party had crossed trails with before. Lifferloss the awakened tree stood in his grove a few blocks toward the town center. They were jolted awake by Orin's shout of "We're under attack!" Fiz reached the window first and, peering into heavy mist, rolled well enough to see the whole raid at once: an NPC — Orin — sprinting from the northern wheat fields with ten goblins, four bugbears, and two ogres at his heels, every one of them hauling a sack of looted crops. Eno's Plant Growth on those fields the day before had been a kindness that rang like a dinner-bell for every hungry thing in the hills. Orin stumbled and fell on the cobbles, and the party piled out to save him.

## The Inn-Yard Brawl

Eno opened by laying a Spike Growth across the lane so the big raiders would have to wade through thorns. The goblins marked him for the caster and buried him under a volley of javelins — eighteen points, breaking his concentration and dropping Spike Growth, then fifteen more that left him bleeding badly. Toz answered with a Tidal Wave that flattened six goblins and a bugbear under falling water, then a Lightning Bolt through an ogre and the goblins behind it, his Heart of the Storm arcing off to bite anything standing near and Tempestuous Magic carrying him ten feet clear afterward. Fiz blessed his companions, set down his Force Ballista, and worked the bugbears with Scorching Ray and Fire Bolt. Hal shoved to Eno's side, healed him with a level-2 Cure Wounds, and traded javelin throws at the front. Naxene threw up Mage Armor, added Fire Bolt and Magic Missile, and landed a Suggestion on a wounded ogre — convincing it the bugbears were the real enemy. Miros bear-hugged a bugbear into the dirt; Orin recovered from the brink, threw daggers from behind the draft horses, then wandered inside to drink a stranger's beer. When the yard went still, one bugbear had vanished into the mist, another hid in a locked house, and the charmed ogre stood frozen in the thorns — left alone so as not to break the Suggestion.

## Pushing to the Town Center

In the lull, Eno shut his eyes, filtered the screaming, and pinpointed two more centers of trouble: the livestock pens being emptied to the east, and a growing fire at the town center where Lifferloss' grove lay. The crew broke off from the hiding bugbear and ran for the grove. They arrived to ruin — two of Lifferloss' treants ablaze with goblins and a bugbear tormenting them, an ogre wrenching a door off a house, and a panicked horse dragging a burning wagon through the alleys. Toz opened with another Tidal Wave that drowned the goblins and doused the burning treants in one sweep, saving them. Lifferloss waded in swinging like siege-beams, even grappling a bugbear and hurling it bodily into another. Hal carved down a bugbear and finished an ogre with javelin and blade. Eno kept up the pressure with Spiritual Weapon, Guiding Bolt, and a Shillelagh-charged staff. Fiz's Fire Bolt and Force Ballista worked the flank. Toz ended it theatrically — a Ray of Frost and then a Thunder Wave that drove the flaming wagon into a failing house and collapsed it atop the ogre, killing it, though an unlucky villager went down under the beams too. Shalvis took a morningstar full in the chest and did not get up. Eno stabilized Lifferloss at the brink, and the old tree woke.

## Bells, Braziers, and a New Alarm

As the last bugbear dropped, the abbey bell began to toll — ding-dong-gong — and the watchtower bells answered from the walls. Braziers flared along every parapet. Every goblin and hobgoblin still loose turned as one and fled toward a single fixed point on the town wall, and the streets fell into a worse-than-screaming quiet. Through it drifted a guard's distant shout: the giants were coming. The raid had never been the attack — it was the errand, thieves sent ahead to fill their sacks and open the way.

## Loose Ends

- Shalvis is dead in the street — the party still doesn't know he was Zhentarim, and Kella is presumably waking to the news.
- One bugbear escaped into the mist and another hid in a locked house near the inn; either could recur.
- The charmed ogre is stuck in the Spike Growth back at the inn (Suggestion lasts up to eight hours).
- Miros was down on death saves and Lifferloss survived only barely; his grove and two treants are scorched.
- Villager and livestock losses are mounting — a collapsed house with a townsperson inside, trampling horses, scattered casualties.
- Undelivered letters still outstanding (Lily in Daggerford, from Orin Greenbow; a second bound for Waterdeep).
- Giants are inbound on Golden Fields, and the crew has already burned through much of their magic on the warm-up.
