## Recap & setup — Golden Fields, and the honeypot they planted

- The DM recaps the prior session: the crew arrived in **Golden Fields** and got a lore dump from **Zephyros** about the **ordning** (the giants' hierarchy), the rampaging giants, and the **Oracle**. The town's guards are lazy and incompetent; there'd been a recent giant attack the town was blasé about. The leadership hierarchy (castellan, captain of the guard) baffles Fiz specifically.
- Prior beats recalled: they met **Miros** (yeti innkeeper) and got a free room; spoke with **Lifferloss** for old history; **Eno** cast a plant-growth ("awaken"/plant growth) spell on the fields — which, per an in-fiction podcast joke, "**fattened up the lamb for the giants**," i.e. created a magical-crop **honeypot** in the wheat fields right beside a town with a hole in its wall and lazy guards. They met the abbot (nonplussed) and his stressed acolyte, and **Naxene** (noble wizard at the inn) who warmed to **Hal** after he charmed her (good Charisma roll), though she remained cool toward elves.
- Undelivered mail noted: a letter to **Lily in Daggerford** from **Orin Greenbow**, and a second letter to **Waterdeep** — the crew "were really bad mail people" and delivered neither.
- **The night alarm:** the party (asleep at the inn, ~5 blocks from the town center) is woken by **Orin the drunken halfling** shouting *"We're under attack!"* Present at the inn: Naxene, Shalvis, and Miros; Lifferloss is out in the town center. **Fiz** goes downstairs first; the whole party arms up and follows.

## Fiz's perception check reveals the raid

- **Fiz** makes a Perception check to see out the window into heavy mist (**disadvantage**, because fog = lightly obscured). Rolls **17 and 19**, takes 17, **+4 = 21** — good enough to see everything clearly.
- He sees an NPC (the fleeing figure the transcript garbles as "Yogulvi" — *attribution uncertain, but this is Orin, who is shown falling and making death saves moments later*) sprinting from the **north wheat fields (the honeypot)**, chased by **10 goblins, 4 bugbears, and 2 ogres** — all carrying **sacks of vegetables / giant magical crops** looted from the grove. The fleeing NPC stumbles and falls on the cobblestones in front of the inn. The party rushes to his aid. **Initiative.**
- DM notes: because Fiz rolled so well, the whole raiding force is visible at once instead of appearing one-by-one out of the mist. Goblins carry shields + scimitars (and short bows, switched to as needed); everyone carries javelins.
