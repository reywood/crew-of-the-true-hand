*In brief: The wrecked crew of the* True Hand *stagger inland to Nightstone, find it overrun by goblins and dire wolves, and end up defending the ruined village alongside a party of adventurers hunting a missing woman named Kella.*

## A bottle from the wreck

The session opened with introductions and a look back at the crew's arrival on this coast. The *True Hand* had been battered to splinters by giant-flung boulders off the shore south of Nightstone — thrown, as it turned out, for sport. Four of the crew survived alongside the party: **Ol' Rourke** the navigator, **Fish Hook Finn** the deck hand, **Liera** of the Pearl Isles who kept the galley, and **Garret "Ox" Dorn**. From the wreckage came an ornate bottle etched in Primordial — *"To speak my name is to unmake chains."* With it came a delivery and a fat fee, promised by a retired adventurer named **Molak** who kept the inn at Nightstone, on the edge of Ardeep Forest.

On the road inland, two goblins demanded a toll. Fiz answered with Catapult, flinging a rock hard enough to end one where it stood — a killing whose ease sat badly with him afterward.

## Nightstone in ruins

A bell was pealing without pause inside the village. The drawbridge lay open, the watchtowers stood empty, and boulder-craters pocked the keep-mound at the river's centre — the same hand that had done for their ship. In the courtyard two hulking wolf-things, later reckoned wargs, were tearing apart a dog.

Fiz used Magical Tinkering to record six seconds onto a pebble — *if you need help, stop ringing the bell for one minute* — and floated it up the tower with a Mage Hand shaped like a little clockwork bird. The bell stopped for exactly a minute, then resumed. Under cover of the noise the crew slipped past the wargs into the temple.

## The temple and the flight

Fiz put an eye to the cracked door and found no beleaguered villager but two goblins — one swinging gleefully from the bell rope, the other turning his own rock over in baffled fascination. The crew weighed a fight and declined it: the bottle was the job, Molak plainly wasn't here, and the bells were covering them. Toz searched the church instead and found a cutaway panel in the altar hiding the priest's stash — a fine pipe, tobacco under a lion seal, twenty silver. Fiz read the seal as the mark of the **Lion Share Mercantile Group**, which kept a store in the village. The temple was **Lathander's**, with **Mielikki's** symbols strewn oddly alongside.

The withdrawal nearly ended the campaign in its first hour. Toz laid down a Fog Cloud; it bought heartbeats and no more. One warg caught their scent at the drawbridge and charged. Fiz sent his clockwork bird to the tower winch, found it took both winches to shift the bridge at all, then worked the second and began cranking it shut a third at a time. Eno was savaged and dropped on the tilting timber, and lay one failed save from dying while the table seriously debated uncorking the genie to save him. Fiz lunged to drag him clear, missed, and went into the moat. It was Hal who swam the water in his armour and brought Eno back with a single point of Lay on Hands — and Toz who put a crossbow bolt through the warg at last, dropping it down the ramp as the bridge slammed shut behind them.

## A second party arrives

Riders came up the road — adventurers led by a man with a small winged snake circling his shoulder, hunting a blonde Zhentarim agent named **Kella** believed hidden in Nightstone or a cave in the Ardeep beyond. The two parties threw in together, dropped the drawbridge, and swept the village room by room. Only now did the bell-tower goblins die: Toz's swing took the first, Hal's javelin and Eno's bolt brought the rope-swinger down out of the air, and the bell fell silent for good. The townsfolk were not here at all. The old goblin woman with the bread did not survive the sweep — one of the riders shot her in the back, picked through her sack, and left it.

## Molak's inn — and orcs at the gate

In the inn a goblin lay dead across a table with a crossbow bolt in its chest that none of them had fired. Upstairs, in what could only be Molak's quarters, hung a tapestry of a mountain landscape beside a heavy locked chest. Finn tried the lock and failed; Fiz tried after him and failed; Hal swung at it and heard something break inside. The chest stayed shut. They took the tapestry, meaning to return it.

Then the shout went up from the watchtowers: an orc warband at the drawbridge, led by a huge and already badly wounded brute with a shaman at his side. The crew took the walls. Hal shoved one climber into the moat, Eno's Shillelagh dropped another, and Fiz burned a third down with Fire Bolt before cracking the chief with a Catapulted rock. Hal was crit to three hit points and Toz dropped outright by a readied javelin volley before Eno's Healing Word brought him back. It was the shaman who ended it, talking her chief into withdrawal. The crew reached level 3, pending a rest.

## What's next

- Find Molak himself — alive, dead, or fled — and hand over the bottle he paid for.
- Get the broken chest in his quarters open, and return his tapestry.
- Follow Kella's trail into Ardeep Forest, toward the cave the riders' captain believes she was taken to.
- The Lion Share Mercantile Group keeps a store in the village, and the priest hid their tobacco under his own altar.
- Work out who is throwing boulders at ships and villages both, and why goblins, wargs, and a battered orc warband all converged on Nightstone at once.
- Learn the name in the bottle before someone under pressure speaks it blind.
