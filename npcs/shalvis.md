A quiet Zhentarim agent the crew found sharing a room at Miros' inn in Golden Fields — sleeping beside Kella, the estranged bounty the party has crossed trails with before. Nobody at the table clocked him for a Zhentarim man until it was too late to matter; to the crew he was just another guest under the yeti's roof. When the night raid hit, he armed up and fought alongside the party's other NPC allies, trading crossbow and quarterstaff blows and proving a little tougher than he looked (~27 HP behind a light 13 AC). In the second wave, at the burning grove by the town center, a bugbear's morningstar crit dropped him, and his death saves ran out in the street — he died there, his secret loyalty going into the dark with him, still unknown to the crew.
