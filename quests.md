# Quests

Active threads, dormant leads, and resolved jobs. Source session in parens.

## Main arc — the giant ordning

Giants of every kind are on the move and attacking settlements across the North. The party is trying to find the Oracle to understand why.

- **Reach the Oracle.** Path is Spine of the World, with the giant **Harshnag** as guide. Need a **giant conch** to call/contact the Oracle; a dragon may be the way to get it. **A giant is needed to open the way** — Kuyrl Stonepalm claims goliath giant-kin qualifies. (2026-03-08, 2026-06-16, 2026-08-12)
- **Copy Kuyrl's map to the Oracle.** Kuyrl keeps a map in his ledger booklet. Trade offered: he copies from Zephyros's book of giant runes, the crew copies his map. Needs cartographer's or navigator's tools. Not yet done. (2026-08-12)
- **Find Harshnag.** Last known to be in Silverymoon. Was a member of the now-defunct adventuring party **Force Gray** out of Waterdeep. Statue of him stands in Waterdeep. (2026-03-08, 2026-06-16)
- **Get to Silverymoon via the Harpers.** Harpers have a secret teleportation portal network. Contact **Yackerty**, a dwarf Harper in the Trades Ward of Waterdeep. Fiz has an old Harper friend from Halruaa as a backup contact. (2026-06-16)
- **Visit the Eye of Annam.** The giant gods want to help but the party must come to them. (2026-02-10)

## Side leads / unresolved

- **Genie bottle.** Salvaged from the *True Hand* wreck. Primordial inscription: "To speak my name is to unmake chains." Need the genie's name. **Derothra Shendrel** in Triboar knows it. (2025-09-23, 2025-12-07)
- **Mind-control attack at the Deep Water Inn.** Patrons' eyes turned white and attacked; the instigator was an ordinary dockhand, **Emmert Dorrow**, and seawater washed out of his body when he died. Every victim had lately dreamt of being called to the ocean — **the same dream Toz has been having**, which Toz is hiding. The Umberlee priestess's verdict: **the work of a warlock** who has tapped the goddess's power. **Find a divination wizard or cleric** to trace them. (2026-06-16, 2026-08-12)
- **"The deep speaker."** The Cult of the Dragon's standing orders instruct their agents: *"do not impede the designs of the deep speaker."* Unexplained, and sitting squarely alongside the ocean dreams, the eye-coin, and the barnacled figure on the docks. (2026-08-12)
- **The barnacled robed figure.** Ambushed the crew in fog on the docks with entranced workers and octopuses; was shocked that a magical blade could hurt it. Killed, sank into the harbour, **body never recovered**. Best candidate yet for the warlock or its servant. (2026-08-12)
- **A dead innocent.** An entranced dock worker was killed by the crew's *thunderwave* during the fog ambush, in a city whose watch already tried to detain them once that day. (2026-08-12)
- **Hal's cursed sword.** Pulled from the sarcophagus in the Nightstone crypt. Diagnosed at the Plinth as a **grave goods** curse with exactly two remedies: return the blade to the Nightstone crypt, or get **permission from a blood relative / heir of the Nandar family**. Lady Nandar was the last known of them. Curse not yet broken. (2025-12-17, 2026-08-12)
- **The slime in the stalactite.** Bailed on killing it in the Nightstone goblin cave. Still there. (2025-12-07)
- **Kella bounty** out of Darkhope. Kella's father is famous in Darkar; Teldor is her grandfather. Unclear if the party is still chasing this. (2025-11-12)
- **Corvin** holding Nightstone. Royal blood, within his rights; Daphne and Xolkin both unhappy about it. Aganor swore an oath to Asmodeus. Left unresolved when the party moved on. (2025-12-17)
- **Naxene's letter to Chazlauth.** Delivered. (handed off 2026-06-16)
