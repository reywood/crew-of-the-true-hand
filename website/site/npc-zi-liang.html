Zi Liang

Mentioned in sessions: 2026-06-02
Based at: Golden Fields
Type: Ally
Location: Golden Fields
Affiliation: Church of Chauntea
First Seen: 2026-06-02

An acolyte of Chauntea serving the abbey at Golden Fields, the great granary-town of the North. When the giants came, she kept her head while the town lost its: she led the crew up into the bell tower for a look at the threat coming over the walls, then broke off to go help the town guards on the wall rather than shelter in the abbey — the crew following her out into the fields. Steady under fire and known by name to the men on the wall.

Tales from the sea and the giant-haunted North.
