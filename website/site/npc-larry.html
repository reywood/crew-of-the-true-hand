Larry

Based at: Waterdeep
Type: Acquaintance
Location: Waterdeep, Dock Ward
Role: Translator
First Seen: 2026-08-12
Expertise: Giant, translation

Brindle's translator on the Waterdeep docks: lanky, silver ponytail, eye patch, quiet and unassuming. He speaks Giant, which is how Brindle gives orders to Kuyrl Stonepalm, who speaks no Common.

When one of the crew addressed him in Giant and bluffed that Kuyrl already knew he was being swindled and was unhappy about it, Larry answered first in Giant — "how does he know that?" — and then flatly, in Common: "What's he going to do about it?" An intimidation attempt failed. He knows exactly what Brindle is doing and is untroubled by it.

Tales from the sea and the giant-haunted North.
