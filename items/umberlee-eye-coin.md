A coin taken from Emmert Dorrow's body at the Deep Water Inn, folded inside a leather pouch in a pocket separate from his purse. One face bears an **eye**. The other bears a **number** — unique to each coin — buried under white-and-green calcification and completely unreadable. Fiz's tinker's tools could not clean it and risked only damaging it further.

**What it does.** It takes hold of anyone who went white-eyed at the inn: the barkeep touched it and blanked, and had no memory of it afterward. It did nothing at all to Fiz, who handled it throughout, nor to a patron who had not been possessed. **It also affects Toz**, who hid the fact — only Eno noticed, and has said nothing.

***Identify* reading.** A magic-imbued object, therefore a magic item. **No attunement, no charges, no useful properties.** Not created by a spell. Exactly one spell is currently upon it: **Eternal Corrosion** — it is continually corroded, and cleaning the corrosion causes it to return magically. Reading it taught Fiz the spell as a cantrip: castable on any object he can touch that fits in the palm of his hand. The DM has confirmed it is **homebrew and its full details are still to be determined**.

**What it means.** The priestess of Umberlee identified it as an old symbol of her goddess — shine and rot at war on one disc, life and death, a cycle. She was clear the coin is **not cursed**; it merely displays the corruption of the sea. What was done with it at the inn was *strange*, and her verdict was **a warlock** who has tapped Umberlee's power for their own ends.

Stowed in the party's Bag of Holding, with instructions that nobody handle it.
