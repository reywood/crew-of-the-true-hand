A set of blue-steel thieves' tools etched with Draconic numerals along each pick. Taken from the Bag of Holding of the kobold who fell at Goldenfields. The tools work as ordinary thieves' tools, and the etchings looked like they hinted at something more — a matched dragon-code, or a specific set of locks they were keyed to.

**Resolved 2026-08-12.** Chazlauth examined them and found nothing hidden: they are **not magical** and the Draconic numerals are purely decorative. He wanted them anyway, purely because they looked incredible, and offered a hundred-and-something gold — roughly ten times an ordinary set's price. The crew asked for an item instead and **traded them for an immovable rod**.
