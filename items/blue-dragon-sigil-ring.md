A non-magical ring bearing the sigil of a blue dragon, taken from the Bag of Holding of the kobold who fell at Goldenfields alongside a young blue dragon and a half-dragon rider.

**Identified 2026-08-12 by Chazlauth:** it is a **badge of membership in the Cult of the Dragon** — a faith in all but name (they do not call themselves one) which worships **Tiamat** and works to advance the aims of chromatic dragons. That places the half-dragon the crew killed, and the standing orders in the Draconic notes he carried, squarely inside the Cult.
